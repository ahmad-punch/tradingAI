# Contributing

This repo starts as one person's plan and is meant to become a shared curriculum (family first, product later).

- Read `docs/00-reality-check.md` before proposing anything. Proposals that make the numbers rosier need a source.
- Changes to rules/strategy: open a decision record first (`decisions/DECISIONS.md`), then change the docs/tools to match.
- Journals and personal trade data are per person. Keep your own under `journal/<name>/` once there is more than one trader; the templates stay at the top level.
- Keep commits plain and descriptive. No AI attribution lines.
