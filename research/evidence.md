# Research: how likely is a self-taught retail trader to become consistently profitable?

Compiled 2026-10-21 by an AI research pass with web search. Where a number is self-reported by a prop firm or comes from a vendor with a commercial stake, it is flagged. Verify anything you act on.

## 1. Academic and regulatory evidence on retail/day-trader profitability

**Taiwan (Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean; later with Zhang).** Complete Taiwan Stock Exchange records, 1992–2006, every day trader in the market.
- 2004 version ("Do Individual Day Traders Make Money?"): in the typical six-month period more than eight out of ten day traders lose money. Heavy day traders earn gross profits but not enough to cover transaction costs. A small group shows persistent skill. https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/papers/Day%20Traders/Day%20Trade%20040330.pdf
- "Learning Fast or Slow" (formerly "Do Day Traders Rationally Learn About Their Ability?", *Review of Asset Pricing Studies* 2020): aggregate net-of-fee performance of day traders is negative in every one of 15 years; each day ~97% of day traders can expect to lose money; predictably profitable traders are under 3% of day traders active on an average day (the 2014 companion paper: under 1% of the day-trading population). Survival: 44% still trading at one year, 24% at two, 15% at three. https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2019/preliminary/paper/ZKnGb4Zh

**Brazil (Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti, "Day Trading for a Living?", 2020).** Everyone who began day trading Brazilian index futures 2013–2015 and persisted 300+ days (~19,600 starters, 1,551 persisters). 97% lost money; ~1.1% earned more than minimum wage; 0.4–0.5% more than a bank teller (~US$54/day); best trader US$310/day with daily SD of US$2,560. "No evidence of learning by day trading." https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101

**US (Jordan & Diltz, *Financial Analysts Journal* 2003).** Seven branches of a US day-trading firm, 1998–99 bull market. About twice as many lost as made money; ~20% "more than marginally profitable". https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research/financial-analysts-journal/2003/the-profitability-of-day-traders

**India (SEBI, Aug 2026).** 7.49M first-year equity-derivatives traders: 90.97% net losers in year one, 94.4% in year two; of those active every year FY22–FY26, 0.5% were profitable in all five. https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/sebi-futures-options-study-retail-traders-trading-losses-experience

**Forex-specific.** Hayley & Marsh (*J. Int. Money & Finance* 2016), retail FX platform data: no evidence retail FX traders learn to trade better; even very experienced traders consistently lose. Cites French AMF study (2009–2012, 14,799 clients): 89% lost money; median loss ≈ €1,843. https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/15691
- US CFTC/NFA quarterly disclosures (profitable = positive P&L that quarter, an easy bar): FOREX.com Q2 2026 32.6% profitable; tastyfx 34.5%. A 2011 SEC comment letter notes ~30/70 holds quarter after quarter, which with survival data implies the 30% are not the same people. https://www.forex.com/en-us/terms-and-policies/risk-disclosure/ ; https://www.sec.gov/comments/s7-30-11/s73011-10.pdf

**ESMA/FCA-mandated CFD warnings.** ESMA 2018: 74–89% of retail accounts lose, average losses €1,600–€29,000. Current: Pepperstone 79.6%; IG 75–78%; IC Markets 73.4% (May 2026); eToro 76–77%; FCA sample of 8 firms: 82% lost, average −£2,200; ASIC FY2024: 68% lost, A$458M net. https://www.esma.europa.eu/node/84933 ; https://brokerchooser.com/broker-reviews/ig-review ; https://asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2023-releases/23-204mr-asic-sues-etoro-in-its-first-design-and-distribution-action-to-protect-consumers-from-high-risk-cfd-products/

**Bottom line:** 65–90% of retail accounts lose in any window; over multi-year horizons the share earning a living is well under 1–3%; no study finds persistence alone produces learning.

## 2. Prop-firm statistics

**FTMO.** Publishes no pass rate. Co-founder Otakar Šuffner, Sept 2024: "nine out of ten people won't succeed"; "We're not giving our traders real money … everything is done through virtual accounts." https://robincap.com/blog/otakar-šuffner-co-founder-ceo-ftmo . Third-party estimates: 7–15% pass Phase 1, 3–10% both phases. FTMO reports $650M+ lifetime rewards and 4.5M+ customers but never the number ever paid or the median payout. Sept 2026: ~$14.4M to ~7,100 traders ≈ $2,030 per reward. https://coinlaw.io/ftmo-statistics/ ; https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/ftmo-adds-200-million-to-lifetime-payouts-in-one-year-as-monthly-rewards-near-15-million/

**Cross-firm dataset (FPFX Tech, vendor to prop firms; 300,000+ accounts, 100,000 traders, 10 firms, 2024).** 14% of traders passed a challenge; ~45% of funded ever got a payout; **7% of all participants ever received a payout**; average payout = 4% of plan size; average spend ≈ $800 per account over ~3 attempts. Vendor data, unaudited. https://www.tradingview.com/news/financemagnates:3a251e333094b:0-exclusive-only-7-of-300-000-prop-trading-accounts-achieved-payouts/

**The Funded Trader (CEO, March 2025).** Pass rate 5–10%; ~20% of funded get a payout; ≈1–2% of all clients ever paid. Same firm had ~900–1,270 traders in a payout queue and settled some claims at 10–30 cents on the dollar. https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/only-1-in-20-traders-pass-prop-firm-challenges-reports-the-funded-trader/

**Topstep (futures, 2025 disclosure).** 16.8% of Combines passed; 51.8% of distinct participants passed at least once (multiple attempts); 33.3% of funded got a payout; 0.71% promoted to a real-capital Live account. https://fortunly.com/statistics/prop-firm-challenge-pass-rate-statistics/

**FundedNext / FundingPips / The5ers.** No audited transparency reports. FundedNext: ~24.8% pass Phase 1, ~43% of those pass Phase 2 (~10.7% overall), firm-relayed. Payout Junction industry 30-day figures: **median payout $1,092, mean $1,410; 78.6% of payouts under $2,000.** https://payoutjunction.com/statistics

**CFTC v. My Forex Funds (Aug 2023).** Alleged the firm was counterparty to nearly all customer trades, used software to worsen fills, used pretexts to terminate accounts, and handicapped the few successful customers; 135,000+ customers, $310M+ fees. **Dismissed with prejudice May 2025 with sanctions against CFTC staff; nothing proven.** It still made "who is the counterparty, what is simulated, how are fills generated, what are real pass/payout rates" the standard diligence questions. https://tradingbusinessreview.com/market-intelligence/my-forex-funds-cftc-case-prop-firms/

**Median funded-trader outcome:** the modal buyer never gets funded; the modal funded trader never gets paid; the modal paid trader receives ~$1,000–$2,000 once. "Stays funded and paid repeatedly" ≈ 1–3% of buyers.

## 3. The economics and the conflict of interest

- FTMO 2023 accounts: revenue $203.9M, operating costs $100.3M. Payouts ≈ 48–55% of revenue; the rest is margin on fees. Revenue is evaluation fees. https://www.tradeinformer.com/prop-weekly/what-ftmo-tells-us-about-prop-firm-finances
- FTMO 2025 net income fell 59% partly because the gold rally "meant prop firms had to make more payouts": when traders win, the firm loses. https://tradeinformer.com/broker-news/ftmo-sees-59-decline-in-profit-as-co-ceo-says-oanda-performance-not-entirely-ideal
- Belgium's FSMA (March 2024) warned specifically about the course → "certificate" → demo-trading pattern: courses "designed to filter out less committed participants while generating revenue"; the certificate is "a diploma issued by the firm itself"; consumers "never execute real trades." https://www.fsma.be/en/warnings/fsma-warns-consumers-against-shadow-investment-game
- Affiliate programs pay ~10–15% of challenge fees. Anyone telling a funded-trader story with a discount code is paid when you buy, pass or not. https://track360.io/blog/prop-firm-affiliate-economics-report-2026
- Sunk cost: FPFX ≈ $800 per account lifecycle; PipFarm poll of 459 clients: average $4,300 spent, 90% buy at 2–5 firms, ~40% ever profitable; 30–40% of failed buyers re-buy within 90 days. https://financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/exclusive-poll-reveals-60-of-prop-firm-clients-lose-funds-investing-4300-on-average

## 4. Time to competence

No rigorous longitudinal survey; "2–5 years" is practitioner folklore. Academic data: Taiwan and Brazil find no learning on average, only selection (losers quit). Among journaling-app users with 90+ days of logs (self-selected, disciplined), 31% are net profitable. Taiwan shows a documented history of net profitability is the one strong predictor of future profit, and profitable traders cut losers and let winners run. 60–70% of challenge failures are drawdown breaches, i.e. position sizing. https://traderssecondbrain.com/guides/trading-statistics-data ; https://hackernoon.com/the-real-reason-traders-blow-prop-firm-evaluations

## 5. Realistic monthly returns for a funded trader

No firm publishes average monthly return of funded traders. FTMO's scaling plan treats 10% net over a rolling 4 months (~2.5%/month) with profit in 3 of 4 months as the *elite* threshold. The pitched 1–1.5%/month is a modest percentage; the problem is assuming $800k of allocation as a starting point. That is the top of most scaling ladders, reached by a tiny fraction after many consecutive profitable months.

## 6. Red flags in "demo → $200 exam → $10k real account → $300k, $12k/month"

1. "Certification exam" is a challenge fee; no firm issues a recognised certification.
2. The "real account" is not real money. FTMO terms (Feb 2026) describe "fictitious capital" and a "reward for the data you generate." https://tradeinformer.com/prop-weekly/why-did-ftmo-switch-from-funded-accounts-to-simulated-rewards
3. Post-2024 context: MetaQuotes forced brokers to drop prop firms serving US clients without licences ("Prop-ageddon"); firms standardised on explicitly simulated accounts. https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/prop-ageddon-is-your-prop-firm-still-online-real-time-updates/
4. Payouts come from company revenue (other buyers' fees), so they depend on the firm's solvency and rule interpretation.
5. Funnel math: ~7% ever paid; 1–3% paid repeatedly; median payout ~$1,100.
6. The storyteller is paid on your fee, not your success.

**Verdict:** Across every large dataset, 70–97% of active retail traders lose, under 1–3% earn a living, and persistence does not produce learning on average. Prop-firm data are consistent: ~5–15% pass, ~7% ever paid, ~1–3% paid repeatedly, median payout ~$1,000–2,000, average spend $800–4,300.
