## 3. Collapses, scams, regulation 2023–2026
- **My Forex Funds:** CFTC alleged ~$310M from ~135k customers (Aug 2023); **dismissed with prejudice May 2025**, CFTC sanctioned. Traders unpaid ~2.5 years regardless.
- **True Forex Funds:** MT licence pulled Feb 2024; insolvent May 2024. **SurgeTrader** closed May 2024.
- **The Funded Trader:** paused Mar 2024 amid payout denials; ~900 traders awaiting payouts; admitted payouts had reached 75–80% of challenge revenue some months.
- **Funded Engineer** (alleged exit scam), **FundedFirm** (payout claims cut 90%), **Indigo Trader Funding** (UK strike-off 2024), **Hola Prime** (~1,300 fake reviews removed 2025).
- **2026:** MyFundedFX/Seacrest ended prop trading Feb 2026; OANDA Prop Trader withdrawn; ATFunded suspended. FM Intelligence estimates up to ~100 prop firms closed early 2024–late 2025.
- **MetaQuotes Feb 2024:** pulled MT4/MT5 from grey-label-backed prop firms over unlicensed US clients; migrations to cTrader/DXtrade/Match-Trader.
- **Regulators:** ESMA/CySEC say retail prop trading "not an immediate priority" (Jul 2026); Consob warning 2024; Belgium FSMA warning Mar 2024.
- **Red flags:** unverifiable registration; anonymous leadership; rule changes after you pass; "compliance review" delaying payouts; 100%-split/very-cheap combos; review bursts dated before launch; payout "proof" without transaction records; no named broker; heavy reliance on resets and promo codes; firm under 12 months old.

## 5. Swing vs scalping under prop rules
- Static balance-based DD (FTMO 2-step, FundingPips Standard, The5ers High Stakes) is neutral. Intraday trailing/equity-based DD (E8 One, FTMO Instant) punishes swing traders because floating losses count. EOD-trailing (FTMO 1-step) is moderate.
- Daily-loss limits are the main threat to swing trades hit by an overnight gap.
- Consistency/best-day rules (30–50%) hurt swing traders (multi-day winner books on one day). FTMO 2-step and The5ers High Stakes have none.
- News windows hit scalpers more; swing traders must check whether *holding through* news is banned.
- Weekend: FTMO Standard closes before weekend (use Swing account); FundingPips suspends weekend holding on funded; The5ers, Alpha, Maven allow it.
- **Verdict:** 2-step evaluations with static DD, no consistency rule, and swing-friendly accounts (FTMO Swing, The5ers High Stakes, FundedNext/FundingPips evaluation phase) suit swing traders. 60–70% of failures are drawdown breaches, not time-outs; ≤1% risk per trade is the consensus.

**Bottom line for Pakistan:** E8 and Topstep exclude Pakistan. FTMO, FundedNext, FundingPips, The5ers, Alpha Capital and Maven appear open; USDT is the practical payment rail; confirm in writing with support before paying; start with the smallest sensible account; test a full fee→payout cycle before scaling.
