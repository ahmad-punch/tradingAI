# Curriculum (Phase 0-1, ~60 hours, $0)

Tick boxes as you go; log each session in LEARNING-LOG.md. Sources and alternatives in research/tooling-and-learning.md §1.

## Phase 0 — Mechanics (≈12 h)
- [ ] Read this repo: docs/00 → 03 (1 h)
- [ ] Babypips Preschool: what is forex, pips, lots, leverage, margin, order types, sessions (4 h) — https://www.babypips.com/learn/forex/school-of-pipsology
- [ ] Babypips Kindergarten + Elementary: brokers, charts, candlesticks basics, support/resistance intro (4 h)
- [ ] Open MT5 demo + TradingView free; place 20 practice orders of every type; take a screenshot of each order ticket (2 h)
- [ ] Read FTMO Trading Objectives page and FundedNext rules page end to end; fill tools/challenge_rules.json (1 h)
- [ ] Quiz yourself on docs/01 glossary without looking (30 min)

## Phase 1 — Strategy foundations (≈48 h)
- [ ] Babypips Middle School: trends, S/R, Fibonacci (skim), moving averages, ATR (4 h)
- [ ] Babypips Summer School + High School: multiple timeframes, trading plan, risk management, position sizing, expectancy (6 h)
- [ ] Adam Grimes, *The Art and Science of Trading* free course, modules on market structure, pullbacks, and "why most patterns are noise" (12 h; verify availability at marketlife)
- [ ] Mark Douglas, *Trading in the Zone* — all (6 h). Write a one-page summary in learning/notes/douglas.md
- [ ] Van Tharp, position sizing + R-multiple + expectancy chapters (4 h). Rebuild tools/position_size.py from the formulas without looking at the existing one
- [ ] Write Strategy v1 rules in docs/04 unambiguously; have your accountability partner mark 10 charts from the rules; count disagreements (2 h)
- [ ] 100-trade hand backtest on TradingView/FX Replay across 4 markets and 2 distinct years; log every trade in journal/BACKTEST-LOG.csv (12 h)
- [ ] Run tools/journal_stats.py; write the Phase 1 gate review in tracking/PROGRESS.md (1 h)
- [ ] Decide OD-2, OD-3, OD-4; log D-005 (1 h)

## Explicitly skipped (for now)
ICT/SMC content, Elliott Wave, harmonic patterns, indicators beyond EMA/ATR, any paid course, any Telegram/Discord signal room, crypto. Revisit only after D-005 and only through a decision record.
