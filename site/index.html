Can you earn from prop-firm trading with no money of your own?

The honest answer, and a place to track whether it is working for you.

Short answer: maybe, but it is slow, it costs a little money, and most people do not make it.

Think of it like an exam you pay to sit. A trading company charges you a fee to take a test on a pretend (simulated) trading account. If you pass without breaking their loss rules, they let you trade a bigger pretend account and pay you a share of the pretend profits in real money. You never put in trading money. But you do pay for every attempt, and most people need several attempts.

The person in the video lost about $3,000, paid $500 for a course, and failed about 15 attempts over three years before he started getting paid. Most people who try this never get paid at all.

A fair target is a first practice-account win after 6 to 12 months, a first payout after 9 to 15 months, and maybe $1,000 to $3,000 a month by month 18 to 24, but only if your practice results prove you are ready. We cap the money we can lose at $600 in the first year, and every step has a go / no-go check.

Yes, it is possible to earn from prop firms with no trading capital; the mechanism is real and payouts are real for the small minority who pass and stay disciplined. No, it cannot be done "without losing anything": challenge fees are the cost of attempts, and the person in the video lost roughly $3,000, a $500 course fee and ~15 failed challenges over three years before his payouts started.

The base rate for self-taught traders reaching consistent profitability is low (single-digit percent), the timeline is measured in years not months, and the $50,000/month ambition requires managing several million dollars of simulated capital at ~1%/month, which only a handful of people on any firm do.

The realistic, defensible target is: first funded account in 6-12 months, first payout in 9-15 months, $1,000-3,000/month by month 18-24 if (and only if) the demo results justify it. Every step has a go/no-go gate so the total cash at risk stays capped (year-1 loss budget proposed at $600, decision D-004; see the Decisions page).

The odds, honestly

Out of 100 people who pay for the exam, about 7 ever get paid even once, and only 1 to 3 get paid regularly. When someone does get paid, the typical payout is about $1,000, which is not much more than a few attempts cost. The average buyer spends around $800 and gets nothing back.

The star in the video is the far end of that line. We only hear from the winners; the many who paid and quietly quit do not make videos.

That is why this plan practises on a free pretend account first, and only pays for an attempt once the practice results show we can pass.

Reading across: roughly 1 in 14 buyers is ever paid, roughly 1 in 50 is paid repeatedly, and the typical payout is about a thousand dollars. FTMO's own co-founder: "nine out of ten people won't succeed." The guest's ~$8k/month is a far-right-tail outcome; the typical outcome of someone who watched that video and bought a challenge is ~$800 spent and nothing back.

Time to competence: no rigorous data. Practitioner folklore says 2-5 years; the academic data says persistence alone does not produce learning. The one strong predictor of future profit in the Taiwan data is a documented history of net profitability, which is exactly what the demo-phase gate demands before any fee is paid.

Return expectations for the competent: 1-3% a month on simulated capital with losing months. FTMO treats ~2.5%/month sustained over four months as the elite threshold for scaling.

$50,000 a month is not a plan. It would need several million dollars of pretend account, for years, with almost no one managing it. Treat it as a daydream, not a target. $50k/month is a thought experiment, not a plan: at 1.5%/month it needs ~$4M of simulated allocation across firms at an 80% split; scaling caps and breach risk make that a 4-6 year project with a tiny probability.

What this site is, and how to use it

  • It is a checklist and notebook for this plan. You tick boxes, write down trades and money, and it shows whether you are really ready for the next step.
  • Your progress lives in this one browser on this one device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • To keep a backup, or move to a phone or laptop, press Export (top right). It downloads a small file. On the other device, press Import and choose that file.
  • To let a mentor or family member look over your shoulder, send them the exported file. They press Import and see exactly what you see.
  • You can share this whole folder with anyone. It holds no personal data until someone types in their own.
  • Use the Plain / Detailed switch at the top to read the short version or the full version with all the numbers.
  • Static HTML + one CSS file + one JS file. No build step, no network calls, works from file://.
  • All state is in localStorage under a single key (tradingai.v1): profile, gates, curriculum, learninglog, trades, backtest, ledger, decisions, openDecisions. It is per browser and per device.
  • Export downloads tradingai-progress-YYYY-MM-DD.json. Import replaces everything in the current browser with the file (it asks first). Use it to move devices or hand a mentor your record for review.
  • Sharing the HTML folder is safe: it contains no personal data. Sharing an export file shares your data; it is plain, readable JSON.
  • Clearing browser data deletes your progress. Export weekly.
