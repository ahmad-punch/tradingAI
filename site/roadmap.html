The roadmap

Six steps, like levels in a game. You cannot honestly move up until the gate at the end of the level is passed, and the gate is always a number, not a feeling. Think of it as a driving test: you do not get the licence because you feel ready, you get it because you did the parking and the roundabout correctly.

Nothing here costs money until Phase 3. If you cannot pass a gate in the free phases, that is a cheap and useful answer.

Six phases. Each has an entry condition, a time box, what you do and an exit gate measured by numbers. You cannot skip a gate. If a time box expires without the gate being met, the phase is extended once; if it expires again, the project goes to the stop rule (docs/05). Cash at risk is $0 through Phase 2; total cash at risk over the whole plan is capped (D-004, proposed $600 in 12 months).

Time assumption: 8-10 hours/week around a day job. Swing trading on daily/4-hour charts needs ~30-60 minutes of chart work per day plus a weekend review; it does not need you at the screen during market hours.

Timeline

What could the money look like?

Picture a budget envelope of practice money that a firm lets you trade. In a good month, trading well, you might grow it by about 1% (one dollar in every hundred). The firm pays you most of that growth, around 80%. Some months you lose and get nothing.

  • A $50,000 practice account: a good month pays you about $500.
  • A $200,000 practice account: about $2,000.
  • A $500,000 practice account: about $5,000.

When could that happen? The first real money, realistically, around month 10 to 14. About $1,000 a month around month 14 to 20. $50,000 a month is not a plan. It would need about $4 million of practice money, and firms cap how much one person can hold. A realistic success story is $2,000 to $10,000 a month after 2 to 3 years. Plenty of people never get there.
