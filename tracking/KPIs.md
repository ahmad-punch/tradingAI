# KPIs (what we measure, in priority order)

1. **Rule adherence %** — trades taken exactly per the written plan ÷ all trades. Target ≥95%. This is the headline; everything else follows from it or doesn't.
2. **Expectancy (R)** — `(win% × avg win R) − (loss% × avg loss R)`. Target ≥ +0.25R forward, ≥ +0.30R backtest.
3. **Max drawdown %** — worst peak-to-trough on the account. Target ≤5% forward (firm limit 10%).
4. **Distance to breach** — at the worst moment of each day, how far from the daily and max loss lines. Target: never under 2%.
5. **Simulated breaches** — count. Target 0 in any 60-day window.
6. **Hours/week** — target 8-10; under 5 for two weeks = pause and re-plan.
7. **Net R / net %** — reported, never targeted above 3%/month.
8. **Cash at risk vs budget** — D-004.

Not KPIs: dollars, number of trades, win rate on its own, how it "felt".
