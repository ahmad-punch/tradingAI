# Post-mortems

One entry per breached account (simulated or real), per month that hit the −4% cap, and per failed gate. Written within 48 hours, before any new trade.

## Template
### YYYY-MM-DD — <account / event>
- **What happened (facts, with the journal rows):**
- **Rule adherence on the trades involved:**
- **Was this variance within the rules, or a deviation?** (This single question decides the next step: variance → continue; deviation → 60 days back a phase.)
- **Root cause (behavioural, not market):**
- **Change proposed (as a decision ID, or "none"):**
- **Cooling-off period observed:** yes/no
