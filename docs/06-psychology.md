# 06 — Psychology: the failure modes in the transcript, and the countermeasures

Everything the guest did wrong is a named, predictable failure. Our job is not to be stronger-willed than him; it is to make each failure structurally hard to commit.

| What happened in the video | Name | Countermeasure in this repo |
|---|---|---|
| Put PKR 5k in, "nothing happening", put 20k more, then 5k + friend's 5k | **Escalation of commitment / sunk cost** | Fixed loss budget (D-004), separate envelope, no mid-year top-ups |
| Joined a paid signal group, then "hidden gems" promising 2-3× a day | **Outsourcing judgement; greed** | Rule 1 in reality check: no signals, ever. Any "gem" = instant red flag |
| Kept buying every dip of delisted coins "waiting for the pullback" | **Hope as a strategy; no stop** | SL set at entry, never widened; rule guard flags trades without SL |
| CGPA 3.8 → 3.55 while watching trades | **Over-monitoring** | Swing timeframes; no charts during work hours; check twice a day max |
| First challenge: told cousin "it'll pass tonight", woke to SL hits, "emotions got disturbed" | **Outcome attachment; counting money before it's made** | Journal in R not $. Don't discuss open trades with anyone. Weekly review only |
| Next Monday: "this week I'll pass both phases", switched to scalping | **Revenge trading; moving the goalposts** | Weekly loss cap halts trading; monthly target is 3% and is a *ceiling*; strategy frozen by decision record — changing it takes a written ADR, not a bad night |
| 15 accounts in 6 months testing new strategies on real challenges | **Strategy hopping; testing with money** | All testing happens in Phase 1-2 for free. Paid challenges run the frozen strategy only. Max 2 paid attempts per budget |
| "3% a month? Who can be that patient?" | **Impatience; disbelief that simple works** | Gates are numeric. The question "is this too slow?" is answered by the math in docs/03, not by feel |
| Two 2-month streaks with no SL → "I'm the king of the market" → big shot → gone | **Overconfidence after wins** | Monthly target reached → risk *drops* to 0.5%. The best month is followed by the most conservative month |
| Only recovered because of mentor sessions and public accountability | **Isolation** | Weekly review is shared with one accountability person (spouse/brother). Later: the community we build |

## Rituals (cheap, effective)

- **Pre-trade checklist** (in the journal row): bias? location? trigger? SL set? size computed? news checked? correlated exposure? If any "no", no trade. Takes 60 seconds.
- **Post-trade, before looking at P&L:** rate rule adherence 0-10. Adherence is the KPI.
- **Sunday review, 30 minutes:** fill [journal/WEEKLY-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md](../journal/WEEKLY-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md). Send it to your accountability person. Update PROGRESS.md.
- **After any breach (simulated or real):** 48 hours of no trading. Write a post-mortem. The transcript shows exactly what happens without this.
- **After any big win:** same 24 hours off. Re-read the overconfidence row above.

## The one sentence

The guest's whole turnaround was: *stop trying to be clever; do the boring thing the experienced person said, every time, and write it down.* Everything in this repo is scaffolding for that sentence.
