# 08 — Teaching others, and the product question

## Sequencing (non-negotiable)

1. **You** pass the Phase 2 gate on demo. Until then nothing here is validated, and teaching unvalidated material to family is how the Sarmaya-style funnel reproduces itself.
2. **You** get a first payout. Now there is one data point and a full record of what it took.
3. **Brother/wife** start at Phase 0 using this repo, with you as mentor. They run the same gates; you review their weekly reviews. Their fees come from their own budget with the same cap.
4. Only if two of three household traders pass the Phase 2 gate does the material move to "product".

Why so strict: the video's host built a business on a $500 course plus a community; his star student is now his employee. The thing that actually worked for the student was *accountability and a rule-guard*, not the course content, which is widely available for free. If we teach, we teach the thing that worked.

## What can be taught, honestly

| Teachable | Not teachable |
|---|---|
| The mechanics, the rules, position sizing, the journal, the gates | An edge. Each person must backtest and own their numbers |
| Rule adherence as the KPI; the rituals | Patience; but the gates make impatience expensive, which helps |
| How to read firm rules and spot red flags | Whether they personally will tolerate losing months |

Expect the household pass rate to look like the industry's unless the rule-guard and gates genuinely change behaviour. That's the hypothesis the product would rest on.

## The product, if it comes to that

**What exists already** (see research/tooling-and-learning.md §6): comparison sites (PropFirmMatch), journals (TradeZella, Edgewonk), risk-manager EAs, FTMO's own analytics, and dozens of course+community businesses in Pakistan/India (Sarmaaya Financials and peers). Crowded in every direction *except one*.

**The gap:** nothing combines (a) a free, honest curriculum with numeric gates, (b) a cross-firm rule engine that refuses trades and shows distance-to-breach, (c) an enforced pre-trade checklist with AI-written weekly reviews, and (d) South-Asia onboarding (eligibility, rails, legality) in one place. Most tools optimise for *passing*; the data says the problem is *not blowing up*, which is a behaviour product, not a signals product.

**Shape:** "Flight school for prop traders." Free tier: the curriculum + gates + journal (this repo as a web app). Paid tier ($10-20/month): rule-guard connected to MT5/cTrader, AI weekly reviews, multi-account dashboard, mentor review queue. Revenue model is subscriptions, **not** affiliate commissions on challenge purchases, because the moment we earn when someone buys a challenge we become the thing the reality check warns about. (Prop-firm affiliates pay 10-15% of fees; that's where the course sellers' money comes from.)

**Market sanity:** industry revenue ~$850M/year (vendor estimate), several million challenge buyers, Pakistan/India a large and under-served slice. Even a few thousand subscribers at $15/month is a real business. But: regulatory grey zone in Pakistan (SBP circular), firms collapse and take the audience's trust with them, and the honest positioning ("most of you will not make it, here's how to find out cheaply") is a harder sell than "Learn to Earn". It is also the only positioning we'd be comfortable putting family's names on.

**AI's role in the product:** the rule engine, the review writer, the curriculum tutor, the screener. Not signals. Ever.

**Decision point:** this becomes a real plan only at Phase 5 (decision OD-8). Until then the "product" is this repo and the tools, used by one household.

## What's possible vs not, summarised for the original question

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can I earn from trading with no capital? | Yes, via prop-firm payouts, at low odds, after ~a year, with capped fee losses. |
| Without losing anything? | No. Expect $100-$600 in fees before a payout, possibly with no payout. Free through Phase 2. |
| $1,000/month? | Plausible success case at month 14-20 with ~$100k simulated allocation. |
| $50,000/month? | Needs ~$4M allocation at 1.5%/month. Multi-year, low-probability, capped by firm scaling rules. Not a plan. |
| Train brother/wife? | Yes, after you've validated the process; same gates; expect the same base rates minus whatever the rule-guard buys. |
| Build a product? | Yes, a behaviour/rule-enforcement product with an honest curriculum; not a signals or course-funnel product. Decide at Phase 5. |
| Involve AI? | Yes for enforcement, review, tooling, teaching. No for prediction. |
