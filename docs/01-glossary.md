# 01 — Glossary: the language in the video, decoded

The transcript is Urdu/Hindi with trading jargon mixed in. Here is every term you need, in the order you'll meet it.

## The business model

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Prop firm** (proprietary trading firm) | In the retail sense: a company that sells paid evaluations ("challenges") and lets those who pass trade a larger *simulated* account, paying them a share of simulated profits in real money. Different from institutional prop firms, which hire salaried traders. |
| **Challenge / Evaluation / "exam"** | The paid test. You pay a one-off fee (e.g. $150-$600) and get a demo account of a nominal size (e.g. $25,000, $100,000). The friend's "$200 certification" is this. It certifies nothing; it is a fee for an attempt. |
| **Phase 1 / Phase 2** ("fz wan, fz tu") | Most firms use a two-step test. Phase 1: make e.g. 8-10% profit. Phase 2: make e.g. 5%. Both phases must respect the loss limits. One-step challenges also exist (one target, tighter drawdown). |
| **Funded account / "live account"** | What you get after passing. At most firms since 2024 it is still a **simulated** account; the firm pays you from its own revenue (mostly other people's challenge fees). The "$10,000 real account" in your friend's story is almost certainly this. |
| **Profit split** | Your share of simulated profits paid in cash, typically 80%, sometimes 90% after scaling. "$1,000 made → $800 to you" as the host says. |
| **Payout** | The cash transfer. Guest shows payouts of $670, $2,462, $3,379, $2,784 across Jan-Mar 2026 on ~$200k accounts, i.e. roughly 1-1.5% a month each. |
| **Scaling plan** | Firms increase the nominal account size (e.g. +25% every 3-4 profitable months) up to a cap, commonly $2M-$4M across accounts. The guest manages "~$800k" across four firms. |
| **Allocation** | The nominal capital you're allowed to trade. "Max allocation" = the firm's cap. |
| **Refundable fee** | Many firms refund the challenge fee with your first payout. |
| **Washed / blown / breached account** | Broke a rule (usually max loss) → account closed, fee lost. The guest "washed 15 accounts" in six months. |

## The rules you can break

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Drawdown (DD)** | How far your equity has fallen from a reference point. |
| **Max daily loss** | Usually 4-5% of starting balance in one day (some firms measure from previous day's balance *or* equity, whichever is higher, which catches people). Breach = account closed. |
| **Max overall loss / max drawdown** | Usually 8-10% from the starting balance (static) or from the highest balance reached (trailing — much harsher). Breach = account closed. |
| **Minimum trading days** | E.g. must trade on at least 4-5 separate days before passing. |
| **Consistency rule** | Some firms require no single day to be more than e.g. 30-40% of total profit. Stops one lucky trade passing you. |
| **News rule** | Some firms forbid opening/closing trades within ±2 minutes (or more) of high-impact news. Guest's first breakeven came from an overnight AUD/NZD news move. |
| **Weekend holding** | Some challenge types forbid holding positions over the weekend. Matters for swing trading. |

## The trading itself

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Forex** | Currency pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/JPY, AUD/NZD). The guest trades these plus likely gold (XAU/USD) and indices. |
| **Spot vs futures** (in the crypto part of the story) | Spot = buy the asset. Futures/perpetuals = leveraged bets. He started with spot crypto, lost on "hidden gems" (tiny tokens on decentralised exchanges that went to zero / were delisted). |
| **Signals / signal group** | Someone else tells you what to buy. He paid for a Facebook/Telegram premium group; lost everything. Universal lesson: never trade someone else's signals with your money. |
| **TP (take profit)** | The price at which a trade is closed in profit. "TP hit" = win. "TP1/TP2" = partial targets. |
| **SL (stop loss)** | The price at which a trade is closed at a loss. "SL hit" = a controlled, planned loss. Not a disaster, a cost of doing business. |
| **Breakeven (BE)** | Moving the SL to the entry price so the trade can no longer lose. His first challenge went to BE on two trades instead of passing. |
| **Risk per trade** | How much of the account one SL costs. He started at 2% (too much for prop rules), later 0.5-1%. |
| **RR (risk:reward)** | Ratio of what you make if TP hits to what you lose if SL hits. At 1:3 RR you can win 3 of 10 trades and still profit — the host's point. |
| **Win rate** | Percent of trades that are wins. Meaningless without RR. |
| **Expectancy** | Average $ or R you make per trade: `(win% × avg win) − (loss% × avg loss)`. The only number that matters long term. |
| **R** | One unit of risk. A trade risking $100 that makes $250 is "+2.5R". We journal in R, not dollars. |
| **Swing trading** | Hold trades for days to weeks, few trades a month, analysed on daily/4-hour charts. Guest: 8-10 trades over two months. What the course teaches; what finally worked for him. |
| **Scalping** | Trades lasting minutes, many per day, on 1-5 minute charts. What he switched to in "revenge mode" and blew 15 accounts with. |
| **Revenge trading** | Trading to win back a loss. The #1 account killer. |
| **Technical analysis (TA)** | Decisions based on price charts (levels, trends, patterns) rather than economic fundamentals. |
| **Strategy / setup** | A fixed, written set of conditions under which you enter, where SL and TP go, and how much you risk. "Strategy One" in the community is their named swing setup. |
| **Lot / lot size** | Position size. 1 standard lot = 100,000 units of base currency. Position size is *derived* from risk % and SL distance, never chosen by feel. |
| **Pip** | Smallest standard price move (0.0001 on most pairs). |
| **Leverage** | Borrowed exposure. Prop firms typically give 1:30 to 1:100. Irrelevant if you size by risk %, dangerous if you don't. |
| **Journal / trade log** | Spreadsheet of every trade with setup, R result, screenshots, notes. Guest credits it, along with publishing calls publicly, for the turnaround. |
| **Mentor / community / alumni** | Sarmaya's Discord. The real value he describes is accountability, not secret knowledge. |
